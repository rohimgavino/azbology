COMPANY PROFILE | 02

ABOUT US

AZBO is a local initiative born from a concern for the quality of herbal products and smallholder agriculture. With that same spirit, we ventured into the world of coffee and created AZBOLOGY, a single-origin coffee line sourced from farmers on the slopes of Mount Sindoro and Sumbing in Temanggung, and in Kalibening, Banjarnegara, Central Java.

It began with farmers who wanted to sell their harvest to send their children to school and provide for their families. We stepped in to collect and process smallholder coffee into a high-quality product.

We don't just sell coffee, we bring stories, blessings, and empowerment into every cup.

Coffee is not just a beverage, but a heritage of Islamic civilization that we manage today, from upstream to downstream, with care and dedication. Indonesia is the world's 4th-largest coffee producer, yet most of its farmers are still not directly connected to fair markets. Coffee was once part of Islamic civilization, AZBO is here to reclaim that role.

AZBO was built by Muslims to manage natural wealth in a way that is halal, thayyib, and sustainable.
